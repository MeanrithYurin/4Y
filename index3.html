Game Rules

  1. Decide who goes first among your group.
  2. On your turn, the card will be revealed. Read the interview question aloud.
  3. You have 1 minute to answer.
  4. If you answer clearly and confidently, your partners can award you a point. (Honor system!)
  5. If you cannot answer, you can SKIP, but it will cost -1 point.
  6. The game consists of 5 random questions. Good luck!
